## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report security issues privately and do not disclose them publicly before the maintainers have had time to review and respond.

When reporting, include:

- A short description of the issue.
- The affected component or service (`client`, `server`, `ml-service`).
- Clear reproduction steps.
- Any proof-of-concept details or payloads that help validate the finding.
- The impact you believe the issue could have on rights protection or ingestion.

Please contact the core maintainers of Team Esc(Reality); at security@sportshield.example to initiate a private security conversation.

## Disclosure Process

1. The report is acknowledged privately.
2. The maintainers reproduce and assess the impact.
3. A fix is prepared and reviewed.
4. The reporter is notified when a release or patch is ready.
5. Public disclosure happens only after the fix is available, unless a different timeline is mutually agreed.
